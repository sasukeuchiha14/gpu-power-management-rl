What's being compared. Both runs train the same small-batch ResNet-50 workload on the same physical GPU, back to back. The without trace pins the clock at the card's own max boost speed — what a GPU does with no power management at all. The with trace hands clock control to a DDQN trained offline on five other GPU models and never shown this exact card during training, reading live power/utilisation/clock telemetry and deciding to raise, lower, or hold the clock every 200 ms. It settles on a lower clock within a few seconds and holds it — power drops, the workload's iteration time doesn't measurably change.
